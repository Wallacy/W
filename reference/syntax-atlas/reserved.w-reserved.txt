W syntax atlas — Research and reserved witnesses

This file is not W source. The checker must not parse it.

Research:
- `.bounded` allocator plans remain a research candidate and are not accepted atlas syntax.
- raw literals with multiple hashes need a scanner extension.

Reserved:
- capture(...) is replaced by the contextual <[...]> capture prefix.
- named type arguments and binding modifiers are not grammar forms.
- URL, stdin, shebang, and ambient package discovery are reserved.
