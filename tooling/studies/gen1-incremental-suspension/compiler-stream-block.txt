RESERVED WITNESS — research candidate for a compiler-owned Stream block;
not parseable, not grammar, and not a public frame or ABI.

some Stream<Item, Failure> observeSuspension(
  capture source: take some Stream<view Item, Failure>,
  capacity: 1,
  prefetch: 0,
  cancellation: explicit,
  cleanup: structured,
  effects: throws Failure,
) {
  emit Result<Item, Failure> item
  yield only item
  view does not escape
}

The compiler owns suspension storage. The source contract owns capacity,
prefetch, cancellation, cleanup, effects, and item-only emission. No public
frame identity, resume token, scheduler handle, runtime metadata, or ABI is
introduced. This witness remains Research until compiler/lowering and
human/model evidence exist.
