IPC1 Research witness: Ipc1MappedIpc bounded mapped channel.

Capacity is mandatory, including an explicit cap0 rendezvous. The carrier is
wire/bytes, not generic Channel<T> storage. Slots use relative offsets and
provider-proved process-shared atomic width, order, alignment, progress, and
wait/wake. The sender owns local bytes before commit; the mapped generation
owns bytes after commit and the receiver materializes a new W owner. A process
crash faults the generation. This witness is reserved text, not W syntax or an
API.
