IPC1 current-design-evidence-gap witness: Ipc1MappedIpc bounded mapped channel.

Capacity is mandatory, including an explicit cap0 rendezvous. The carrier is
wire/bytes, not generic Channel<T> storage. Slots use relative offsets and
provider-proved process-shared atomic width, order, alignment, progress, and
explicit cross-process wake receipts. ATOM2 contributes only a value carrier;
address-free process sharing and wake remain provider facts. The sender owns
local bytes before commit; the mapped generation owns bytes after commit and
the receiver materializes a new W owner. A materialization OOM leaves the
committed slot full and makes no partial owner mutation. Panic or process
crash faults the generation; cleanup remains close, unmap, and provider
release. This witness is reserved text, not W syntax or an API.
